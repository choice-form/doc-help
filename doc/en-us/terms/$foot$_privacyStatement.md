# Privacy Policy

Thank you for using the products, services, websites, and applications (collectively referred to as "Services") provided by Shanghai Choiceform Network Technology Co., Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as "the Company," "Choiceform," or "we"). We offer various services, including online survey creation, distribution, collection, statistics, analysis, and other related services such as survey questionnaire services.

This Privacy Policy is intended to inform you about how we collect and process your personal information when you visit our official website www.Choiceform.com (referred to as "the Website"), use our other services and related features, participate in our activities, interact with us through other means, provide products or services to us, or establish other collaborations. Unless otherwise specified, this Privacy Policy applies to all services provided by Choiceform. It explains how we collect and process your personal information, how we protect your personal information, and how you can exercise your rights regarding your personal information.

In this Privacy Policy, "Personal Information" refers to various information recorded electronically or by other means that can be used alone or in combination with other information to identify a specific individual or reflect specific activities of a natural person. This includes but is not limited to, names, mailing addresses, residential addresses, account information, financial status, and location history. It does not include information that has been anonymized. Personal Information also includes sensitive personal information as defined by applicable laws and regulations.

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please feel free to contact us using the contact information provided in the "Contact Us" section of this Privacy Policy.

## Cookie and Similar Technologies Notice:

Like the majority of internet services today, our website and our partners use cookies and similar technologies on the website. We use some cookies that you consent to when using our website, but there are also cookies that are necessary for providing and optimizing our services.

Cookies are small pieces of data stored on your device when you use our services. They help us identify different users. Each cookie has an expiration period, which varies depending on its purpose.

We use cookies and similar technologies for the following reasons:

- To make the website more user-friendly: If the creator uses the "remember me" feature when logging into their account, we store their username in a cookie to speed up the login process when they return to Choiceform.
- For security purposes such as authentication verification: We use cookies to verify your identity, confirm if you are currently logged into Choiceform, and determine if you are affected by any events.
- To improve our services: We analyze and research information, such as your usage of the website, using cookies, and periodically show you different versions of content. This information helps us develop and improve our services, enhance the user experience, and optimize the content we display to you (including promotional content).
- To collect statistical data on the survey response experience: We collect data on whether respondents leave and return to complete a survey, whether they skip parts of the survey, and the time it takes to complete the survey. We also collect other behavioral preference data related to completing surveys. However, this data is only checked and stored in an aggregated and anonymous manner.
- To improve efficiency: Cookies help avoid unnecessary server load and improve service efficiency.
- To provide personalized user preference settings: We store user preference settings, such as your preferred language, interface layout, user-customized content, browser information, and your profile information (including the extent of using the service and the web pages you have visited on our website), to personalize the content displayed to you.

You can choose to remove or disable our cookies through your browser settings.
For more information about the cookies we use, please refer to our "Cookie Policy".
